# Gotchu

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People maintaining a business application who need to understand security observations and decide what to inspect next; technical operators read the underlying evidence. This audience is inferred from Jenn's brief and the existing application, not a completed buyer-research study.

## Product Purpose

Assess explicitly authorized source snapshots and HTTPS origins. Retain evidence-backed reports and distinguish observations, confidence, exploitability and unknown coverage. Help a person decide what to review without presenting a limited assessment as a security guarantee.

## Operating Context

The existing dashboard supports authentication, workspaces, applications, DNS-based authorization, source snapshots, queued assessments, history and report evidence. A gated controlled-demo dashboard compares known fixture revisions. Both operate in observation mode. Repository documentation remains authoritative for authorization and backend capabilities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

TanStack Start, React, Auth.js, pnpm, Node 24 and strict TypeScript are established repository choices. Preserve HTTP/worker/provider boundaries, tenant isolation and authorization. Source is not executed. Severity does not grant permissions. Automated remediation, continuous monitoring and recovery are not delivered by this landing. No mandatory paid identity, model or repository service.

**Current iteration: public landing at `/` plus Impeccable product/design context, guidelines and verification.** `/dashboard`, `/demo`, customer workflows and their styles remain unchanged. No public deployment, waitlist, contact database or invented purchase flow.

## Brand Commitments

Jenn's supplied cybersecurity prototype informs the palette: magenta #E7147D, graphite #191923, off-white #FFFEFA and silver #C7BFBD; futuristic, digital, professional typography and precise controls. Its composition and assets are not a template to reproduce. The current direction is a working brand board: the `[◆]` scope mark, giant wide display type, drenched magenta panels, a blueprint grid, ASCII and dot-matrix art, and nothing realistic (no photos, metal, glass or camera-like objects), without an initial loader. The user supplied a brand board, firecrawl.dev and exa.ai as references for type, elements and feel; colours stay as above. The cream/coral Zcash editorial identity is a different project and is not applicable.

Gotchu is the repository/application name; the reference's earlier “Prototipo” label is not retained. AIRNT is a visual reference, not a licensed commercial web asset. Goldman is the self-hosted OFL display face chosen as the closest open match to AIRNT; do not distribute AIRNT without license evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

Jenn's supplied `prototipo.html` and its reviewed second iteration establish visual direction, not proof of implemented continuous monitoring or remediation. Existing contracts and workflows establish actual scope. No fabricated customers, testimonials, certifications, performance figures or security guarantees. Do not fabricate assessment results. Use authored assets and product-structure panels instead of invented records or pseudo-results, and omit meta/helper captions that explain the artwork or the page. The landing explicitly covers snapshot revisions/digests, DNS authorization, evidence fields, coverage, workspace/application organization, queued-job history and JSON export.

## Product Principles

- Put evidence beside its limits.
- Require explicit scope before assessments; visual emphasis never expands authority.
- Keep the next step legible on a phone and at a desk.
- Use concrete Spanish; explain what a person can do rather than promise protection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Spanish interface with visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, native disclosure, readable contrast, touch-sized controls and reduced-motion support. Automated accessibility checks are evidence for tested states, not whole-product certification.
