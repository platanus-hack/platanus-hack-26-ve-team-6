# Correlation evaluation corpus

Corpus `1.0.0` contains authored ground truth in `cases.ts`; labels were recorded before running the rules. `tuning/` and `holdout/` are separately constructed partitions with at least 14 cases each. Holdout uses different service/environment names, providers, regions, components and windows, and its inputs are checked for digest overlap with tuning. It also includes boundary values, multiple measurements and probes, and all listed acceptance scenarios. Do not tune rules against holdout results. Any holdout misses are reported as observed and must not be used to select thresholds or rule changes.

The report is reproducible with `pnpm --filter @gotchu/triage eval`. Precision/recall are micro-averaged over the five hypothesis kinds within each scenario. A false alarm is a predicted supported cause absent from that case's labels. Abstention counts cases with no supported cause. Confidence errors are counted only where a case has an explicit expected ordinal confidence; the denominator is shown. Impact accuracy is reported by scenario and split with denominators. The report lists every case with an impact or supported-cause mismatch.

Acceptance thresholds are targets for future evaluation, not measured production guarantees: global status can support a provider cause only with a compatible failing route and never above medium confidence; stale series are excluded while fresh probes still determine impact; healthy dependent routes and failing controls count against provider-specific attribution; and high confidence requires a regional/component match, a compatible failing route and a healthy independent control. Holdout scores may be below 100%; preserve and explain every miss without tuning rules against that split. This corpus is small and synthetic and does not establish calibration, causality, production quality, or time-to-decision.
